How the model works

Stage 1: The home team is favored in the latest DraftKings moneyline, and its implied win probability increased versus the earliest saved snapshot that day.
Stage 2: The home team's moneyline handle % ≥ bets % AND spread handle % ≥ bets %. Missing or unmatchable splits are PENDING, not PASS.
Stage 3: Greg Peterson's maximum lay must cover the latest DraftKings home spread. If his line cannot be matched reliably, the final result is PENDING.

Percentages indicate distribution of bets and dollars, not proof of sharp action. Historical snapshots are not live betting quotes. Home/away mismatches are intentionally left pending. This page is informational, not a recommendation to wager.

Source: Public GitHub data repository. Updated on demand from published files.
